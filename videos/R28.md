**YouTube Shorts title:** Do you really need eight glasses of water a day?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Eight glasses of water a day for everyone? Myth. A 2022 study in Science measured daily water turnover in 5,604 people (aged 8 days to 96 years) and found needs vary a lot: physical activity was the biggest factor, and hot, humid weather and altitude raised them too. The researchers said a one-size-fits-all rule is not supported, and that the usual 8-glass advice is probably too high for most people in most situations. The US National Academies say about a fifth of the water you take in comes from food, that they give no "number of glasses" rule, and that healthy people meet their needs by letting thirst be their guide. On a hot day, during exercise, or when pregnant or breastfeeding, you need more. If you have a medical condition or a doctor's fluid advice, follow that. Education, not medical advice. 💧
