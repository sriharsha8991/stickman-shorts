**YouTube Shorts title:** Does your AC cost you more fuel than open windows?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Does your car's AC cost you more fuel than driving with the windows open? Half true. The US government's fuel-economy site says that in very hot weather the AC can cut a normal car's mileage by more than 25%. Open windows cost fuel too, because they add wind drag: in an Oak Ridge National Laboratory test, all four windows down cut fuel economy by 4–8.5% in a small sedan and 1–4% in an SUV. At city speeds the AC usually costs more. At highway speeds the drag from open windows grows, and in that test the gap closed or flipped (above about 100 km/h with the AC at half load, the sedan used more fuel with the windows down). The official tip: windows down at lower speeds, AC at highway speeds. The exact speed depends on the car, the heat and how hard the AC works, and in heavy heat or dust, comfort and safety come first.
