**YouTube Shorts title:** Brushed your teeth? Before you rinse, does water ruin it?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Should you rinse your mouth with water after brushing? Half true that it hurts. In a 2001 test, rinsing with a lot of water left much less fluoride in the mouth. But a three-year school study in Lithuania found children who rinsed and children who only spat got nearly the same amount of new decay (6.8 vs 6.2 tooth surfaces), and both far less than a school without supervised brushing (12.4). Rinsing still washes fluoride away and skipping it costs nothing, so the NHS says: spit, don't rinse. Education, not medical advice. 🪥💧
Sources: Sjögren & Melin, Gerodontology 2001;18(1):15–20 · Machiulskiene, Richards, Nyvad & Baelum, Caries Research 2002 (PMID 12399689) · NHS, How to keep your teeth clean.
