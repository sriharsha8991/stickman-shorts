**YouTube Shorts title:** Does lightning never strike the same place twice?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Do you believe lightning never strikes the same place twice? The US National Weather Service says the Empire State Building is hit about 23 times a year. Tall, pointed towers get hit again and again, and trees and mountains are struck many times a year. So the saying is a myth. When you hear thunder, go indoors, and never shelter under a tree. ⚡
