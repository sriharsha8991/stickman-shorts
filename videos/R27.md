**YouTube Shorts title:** Do dates make you waste your food before it's bad?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Is food bad after its best-before date? Myth. US food agencies (USDA and FDA) say a "best before" or "best if used by" date is about quality, not safety. Food stored as the label says should still be safe after the date until it shows signs of spoiling: an off smell, taste or texture. It may just be a little less fresh. Confusion over date labels causes about a fifth of the food thrown away at home (FDA estimate). Two exceptions: a "use by" date IS about safety (meat, fish, ready meals): never eat food after it. And infant formula must not be used after its date. Education, not food-safety advice for a specific product. 🗓️🥫
Sources: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service, "Food Product Dating" · FDA, "Working with the Food Industry to Reduce Confusion Over Date Labels" · Codex General Standard for the Labelling of Prepackaged Foods (CXS 1-1985), §2 · Food Standards Australia New Zealand, "Best-before and use-by dates".
