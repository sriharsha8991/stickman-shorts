- **Accuracy notes:** Radvansky, Krawietz & Tamplin 2011: memory errors were higher right after walking through a doorway than after the same distance inside one room (virtual rooms and a real lab replication). McFadyen et al. 2021 (four experiments) found the effect only when participants also had a working-memory load (counting backwards), not in plain VR or real walking: said on screen as "It only showed up when minds were busy." The slate payoff "go back through the door" was dropped: in Radvansky 2011 returning to the original room did not restore memory. "Carry one thought, not three" is a tip that follows from the 2021 result, not a tested intervention. ✗/✓ counts in the lab lanes are an illustration (labelled).
