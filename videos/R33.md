**YouTube Shorts title:** Cold car? Before you drive, must it warm up?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Do you need to warm up your car before you drive? For a modern car, a long warm-up is a myth. The US Department of Energy says that even on the coldest days, most manufacturers recommend driving off gently after about 30 seconds. Driving warms the engine faster than idling, and the part that cleans the exhaust starts working sooner. Idling just burns fuel: Natural Resources Canada says ten minutes of idling burns 0.25 to 0.5 litres. So start, wait about half a minute, and drive gently for the first few minutes. Always follow your own car's manual. 🚗❄️
Sources: US DOE, "Consumer Guide to Reducing Vehicle Idling" (2021) · US DOE Alternative Fuels Data Center, "Idling Personal Vehicles" · fueleconomy.gov winter driving tips · Natural Resources Canada, AutoSmart fact sheet 3.
