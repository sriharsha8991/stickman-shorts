**YouTube Shorts title:** Empty charger? Before you unplug it, is it costing you?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Does a phone charger left in the socket, with no phone on it, push up your electricity bill? It's a myth for modern chargers. EU rules (since 2020) and US rules cap the idle power of a phone charger at 0.1 watt. Left plugged in all year, that is 0.88 kWh, under one unit of electricity, about what a 9-watt LED bulb uses in four days left on. Older chargers were allowed more (the EU limit was 0.5 W in 2010, and there was none before), and a charger that feels warm with nothing attached is wasting power, so unplug those. 🔌💡
Sources: Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1782, Annex II · US DOE external power supply standard, 10 CFR 430.32(w) · Commission Regulation (EC) No 278/2009, Annex I.
