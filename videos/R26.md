**YouTube Shorts title:** Does your fridge waste your bread before you eat it?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Does the fridge make your bread go stale faster? True. In a 2011 lab study, bread was stored at room temperature (25 °C), in the fridge (4 °C) and in the freezer (−18 °C). The starch went stale (recrystallised) fastest at fridge temperature. In the freezer, staling almost stopped. Stale is not the same as spoiled: the fridge does slow spoilage, which is why it feels right. If you won't finish a loaf in a few days, or the weather is hot and humid, freeze it in slices and toast them straight from frozen. Exact times depend on the bread and your fridge. Everyday food tips, for education. 🍞❄️
Sources: Aguirre, Osella, Carrara, Sánchez & Buera, "Effect of storage temperature on starch retrogradation of bread staling", Starch/Stärke 63(9):587–593 (2011) · D.
