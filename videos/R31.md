**YouTube Shorts title:** Leftover rice? Before you reheat, can it make you sick?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Can reheating leftover rice make you sick? Half true. Raw rice can carry a germ called Bacillus cereus, and its spores survive cooking. If cooked rice sits warm for hours, the spores wake up, grow and can make a toxin, and reheating does not destroy that toxin (Ireland's food safety authority notes it survives 126 °C for 90 minutes). So the danger is not the microwave, it is the waiting. Cool cooked rice fast (ideally within an hour; a shallow box helps), keep it in the fridge, eat it within 24 hours, and reheat it only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Education, not medical advice. 🍚
