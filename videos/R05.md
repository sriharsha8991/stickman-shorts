**YouTube Shorts title:** Does sugar really make kids hyper?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Before you blame the sugar, are your kids hyper? A 1995 JAMA review pooled 23 blind tests: children got sugar or a sweetener, and nobody knew which. Their behaviour and test scores showed no clear difference. In another test, every boy got a sweetener, but mothers who were told "sugar" rated their sons as more hyper. Caveat: the researchers could not rule out a small effect in some children, and lots of sugar is still bad for teeth and health. 🍰
Education, not medical advice.
Sources: Wolraich, Wilson & White (1995), JAMA 274(20):1617-21, "The effect of sugar on behavior or cognition in children: a meta-analysis" · Hoover & Milich (1994), Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology 22(4):501-15, "Effects of sugar ingestion expectancies on mother-child interactions".
