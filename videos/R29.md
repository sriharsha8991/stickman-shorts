**YouTube Shorts title:** Is brown sugar healthier than white sugar?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Is brown sugar healthier than white sugar? Myth. Brown sugar is either less refined or, often, refined white sugar with a little molasses added back. Per 100 g it has about 380 calories against 387 for white sugar (USDA), so about 2% fewer. It does carry a bit more calcium, iron and potassium, but the amounts are tiny: a teaspoon gives under 1% of the 1,000 mg of calcium an adult needs each day. Your body treats both as added sugar. If you like the taste of brown sugar, that is a fine reason to pick it, just not a health reason. Education, not medical advice. 🍬
