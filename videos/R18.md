**YouTube Shorts title:** Does a bag of rice really dry a wet phone?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Wet phone? Before you grab the rice, does it work? In a 2014 test, wet sponges sat for a day in seven drying materials. Plain uncooked rice soaked up the least water of all seven, and a sponge left in open air dried far better than any of them. Apple's own support page now says: "Don't put your iPhone in a bag of rice. Doing so could allow small particles of rice to damage your iPhone." What Apple suggests instead: tap the phone gently with the port facing down, then leave it somewhere dry with some airflow for at least 30 minutes, up to a day. The test used sponges, not phones, and a soaked phone may still need a repair shop. 📱🍚
