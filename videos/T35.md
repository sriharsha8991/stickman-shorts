**On-screen sources:** Brown, 1991 review (Psychological Bulletin): adults say it happens about once a week. D'Angelo & Humphreys, 2015 (Cognition), 6 lab tests with hard, rare words: when people found the word themselves, it got stuck less on a later test; first-letter hints helped in the same way. A small help, not a cure.
