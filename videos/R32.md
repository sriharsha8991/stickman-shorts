**YouTube Shorts title:** Skipping MSG? Before you do, does it cause headaches?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Does MSG give you headaches? Mostly a myth. In blinded tests where people ate MSG with a meal, it did not cause more headaches than a dummy. In a 130-person trial of people who said MSG made them ill, the few reactions did not repeat when they were tested again. Glutamate is also found naturally in foods like tomatoes and cheese. The one caveat: big doses of 3 grams or more on an empty stomach bothered a few sensitive people, while a normal serving of food with added MSG has under half a gram. If a food reliably makes you feel unwell, talk to a doctor. Education, not medical advice. 🍜
Sources: US FDA, "Questions and Answers on Monosodium glutamate (MSG)" · Geha et al. 2000, Journal of Nutrition 130(4S):1058S · Obayashi & Nagamura 2016, Journal of Headache and Pain 17:54.
