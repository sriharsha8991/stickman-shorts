**YouTube Shorts title:** Does dark mode really save your phone's battery?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Dark mode? Before you trust it, does it save battery? In 2021, Purdue University engineers measured six of the most-downloaded Android apps on four phones (Pixel 2, Moto Z3, Pixel 4, Pixel 5). At 100% brightness, switching from light mode to dark mode saved about 39–47% of the power on average. But at 30–50% brightness, which is where auto-brightness usually keeps your phone indoors, it saved only 3–9%. The saving only exists on OLED screens, where black pixels switch off; LCD screens keep their backlight on whatever the colour. So dark mode is a real battery saver in bright sunlight at full brightness, and a small one the rest of the time. The study measured the screen's power in six apps on 2017–2020 phones, so your own gain depends on your phone and how you use it. 🌙🔋
Sources: Purdue University news release, "Dark mode may not save your phone's battery life as much as you think, but there are a few silver linings" (28 Jul 2021) · Dash & Hu, "How much battery does dark mode save? An accurate OLED display power profiler for modern smartphones", ACM MobiSys 2021.
