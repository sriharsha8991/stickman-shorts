**Description / Reels caption:**
Before you shave again: does hair really grow back thicker? In a 1970 test, five men shaved one leg every week for months. The hair came back just as thin as on the unshaved leg. The stubble only feels rough because the razor leaves a blunt tip. 🪒
Education, not medical advice. Small study (five men, legs only).
