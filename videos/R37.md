**YouTube Shorts title:** Never restart your phone? Are you making hacking easier?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Should you restart your phone? True, it helps. The US National Security Agency's phone guide says: power your phone off and on every week. Why: some spy tools are not saved on the phone at all. They live only in its working memory (Amnesty International found this with Pegasus spyware in 2021), so a restart wipes them and the attacker has to break in again. It is not a cure: install updates quickly and don't open unknown links. 📱🔄
Sources: NSA, Mobile Device Best Practices (2020) · Amnesty International, Forensic Methodology Report: How to Catch NSO Group's Pegasus (2021).
