**YouTube Shorts title:** Wet hair? Before you step out, will you catch cold?

**Description / Reels caption:**
Can going out in the cold with wet hair give you a cold? Half true. Colds come from viruses (more than 200 kinds, CDC), not from cold air, and a 1968 trial that chilled volunteers given a cold virus found no effect. But in a 2005 Cardiff trial, 13 of 90 people who sat with their feet in cold water reported a cold within five days, against 5 of 90 who did not. The likely reason: chilling may help a virus you already carry take hold, and lab work shows the cold virus copies better in a cool nose. No virus, no cold. Education, not medical advice. ❄️🤧
Sources: CDC, About Common Cold · Douglas, Lindgren & Couch, N Engl J Med 1968;279:742–747 · Johnson & Eccles, Family Practice 2005;22:608–613 · Foxman et al., PNAS 2015/2016 (Yale).
