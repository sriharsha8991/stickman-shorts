# H16 storyboard — beat map + shot list (promise → build → reveal)
Series: Myth check · 37.9 s · ONE world ~2.5 screens wide, one camera rig (hold → move 14–30 f → hold):
Place A x≈0–1250: tangerine home office (navy desk, laptop YOUR JOB, phone on a stand, wall clock, window, plant). Door x≈1290.
Place B x≈1360–2700: mint TEST ROOM (two pine desks, blue GROUP 1, orange GROUP 2, one stopwatch each).
Motion language: Playful but calm. No power-up, no transition: one continuous world. Tangerine + mint differs from V023 (butter + rose).

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2–3.5 | Hook | "Your phone pings. Did you just lose 23 minutes?" Frame 0: phone ringing with a 23 MIN price tag, Spark recoils. Overlay LOSE 23 MINUTES? |

| 7.3–9.1 | Context | "That number means something else." MED; the 23 MIN tag flies from the phone to the wall clock. |
| 9.5–12.6 | Layer | "After a ping, people often do two other jobs." Phone pings; OTHER JOB 1 + 2 pop with arrows from YOUR JOB. |
| 13.1–16.2 | Clue | "Then they return, about 23 minutes later." Green arrow back to the laptop; clock wedge fills to 23 minutes; TIME TO RETURN. |
| 16.2–17 | Pause + place change | Spark runs through the door; camera travels; the voice names the test right away. |
| 17.0–20.0 | Test | "In a test, two groups did the same email job." GROUP 1 / GROUP 2, SAME EMAIL JOB, envelopes, stopwatches start. |
| 20.5–22.5 | Problem | "Only one group got interrupted." RING! / HEY! bubbles around group 2, label → INTERRUPTED. |
| 22.5–23.5 | Pause + music drop | Room dims a little, wide shot, WHO FINISHES FIRST? |
| 23.5–25.7 | REVEAL 1 | "The interrupted group finished faster." Group 2's stopwatch dings, FINISHED FIRST. Spark recoils. |
| 26.1–27.9 | REVEAL 2 | "Their work was just as good." Two checked papers, JUST AS GOOD. Spark celebrates. |

| 32.0–34.7 | Action | "At your desk, try one hour with no pings." Dash back, phone laid face down, 1 HOUR, NO PINGS, progress bar fills. |
| 35.1–36.9 | Question + loop | "Did a ping stress you today?" CU Spark, bubble; last frames: phone rings again (frame 0). |
