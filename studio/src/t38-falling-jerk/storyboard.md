# T38 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #10, slate v4)
Format STORY · 27.8 s · ONE world (a night apartment building in cross-section, Spark's bedroom bottom-middle), one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent: amber (the dream rug, the jolt marks, the lights that click on). Spark in full colour in bed; Mr. Trick stands on the nightstand and pulls the rug in Spark's dream. Light: a warm wall lamp in Spark's room; the other rooms are dark until their sleeper jolts.
Motion language: Playful, calm (bedtime). No power-up, no transition.
