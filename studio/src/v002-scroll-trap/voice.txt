Why do you lose hours to your feed every night?
Nobody told you what got deleted in 2006.
Web pages used to have a bottom.
A next page button. A chance to stop.
Then infinite scroll deleted it.
No bottom. No reason to stop.
And pull to refresh?
Sometimes nothing. Sometimes a jackpot.
Random rewards are the hardest to quit.
Its inventor called it addictive.
The infinite scroll guy?
He guesses it eats a hundred thousand lifetimes.
Every. Single. Day.
So put the bottom back.
Phone in another room. Timer on the app.
Or see you again at two forty-seven.
