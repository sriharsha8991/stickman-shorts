Cash versus UPI.
Why does a five hundred rupee note hurt to hand over,
but a five hundred rupee tap feels like nothing?
Round one: cash.
You count out the notes. Every one stings.
Researchers call it the pain of paying.
Round two: UPI.
Tap. Ding. Done. Zero pain.
A two thousand eight study: people offered more for a meal
when paying by card, not cash.
A twenty twenty-four survey: three in four UPI users
say they spend more because of it.
So who really wins?
Cash. That sting was protecting your wallet.
Put the sting back.
Once a week, scroll your UPI history.
