# T01 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #8, slate v4)
Format PLAY · 35.2 s (read test, cap 40 s) · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent: magenta on the word SLEEP (the word that was never there). Spark in full colour sitting up in bed; Mr. Trick by the list cord.
Motion language: Playful, calm (bedtime). No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 0 | CU Spark in bed, smug, arms crossed, lamp light, rolled list above with its cord; overlay WATCH THESE WORDS |
| 0.2–3.0 | Hook | "Why might you remember one word you never saw?" Taps his head on "remember". |
| 3.8–5.3 | Promise | "Wait for the last part." Pull back: the bedroom, the lamp, Mr. Trick walks in to the cord and tips his hat. |
| 5.8–8.5 | Build | "Read these words, and try to remember them." Mr. Trick pulls the cord (crouch, tug), the list unrolls with a small overshoot; camera onto the list. |
| 7.4–15.5 | TEST | Fully still picture (no camera, no Spark, still pencil lines): the 15 words. |
| 15.5–17.3 | Question / first payoff | "Was the word sleep on the list?" List rolls up; Spark's thought bubble fills BED, DREAM, PILLOW, SLEEP (magenta on "sleep"). |
| 17.4 | Spark fooled | YES! bubble, smug nod, bulb flickers. |
| 18.3–20.6 | ANSWER | "Look again, because it was never there!" List rolls back down; half-second music drop; hit on "there!" (20.2 s): SLEEP cracks, falls and smashes on the blanket (20.8 s). SLEEP WAS NEVER THERE. Spark shock → sad. |
| 21.8–24.9 | REVEAL | "Your brain saw bed and dream, and it added sleep." Camera pushes in. BED and DREAM circled, magenta lines into Spark's head, then thin lines from every word; a dashed SLEEP draws in the bubble. Biggest chord on "sleep." (24.5 s): mindblown, bulb lights. YOUR BRAIN ADDED IT, held to 25.5 s. |
| 25.5–31.5 | Proof | "In one test, people heard lists like this." Camera to the TV on the dresser: A MEMORY TEST, two students with headphones, speaker waves. "About half the time, they still remembered sleep." ABOUT HALF, papers fill: one shows SLEEP. |
| 32.3–34.1 | Question | "Did you see sleep on the list?" Back to Spark's face, double point + wink; the list rolls up. |
| 34.1–35.2 | Loop | Smug, arms crossed, WATCH THESE WORDS = frame 0. |

Promise check: "Wait for the last part" pays off with the reveal that the remembered word was never shown and why. True.

## Spark acting
arms crossed, smug → taps head → arms crossed, curious → point up, wink → hands on hips, determined → lean in → chin, thinking (frozen during the test) → present, happy → point up, excited → arms crossed, smug + nod (YES!) → hands on hips → lean in, worried → recoil + hop, shock → point down, sad + head shake → point at BED, curious → point at DREAM → chin, thinking → mindblown + hop, bulb on → point at the TV, curious → shrug, skeptical → present both, laugh → lean in → double point, wink → arms crossed, smug.

## Sound
Bedtime lullaby, Ab major, 80 BPM: harp arpeggios, soft string pad, upright bass, low clarinet. Harp run as the list unrolls; near silence during the test; plucked steps under the question; clarinet slide on YES!; half-second drop; timpani + brass + glockenspiel on "there!"; glockenspiel tumble as the word smashes; harp glissando and the biggest chord on "added sleep"; clarinet tune at the TV; harp back to frame 0, ends on Ab. SFX: page (unroll), pops (bubble words), ding, impact + stamp, shatter, scribble circles, swirl, TV clink.
