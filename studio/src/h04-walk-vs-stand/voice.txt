You stand after lunch? Stop.
A two-minute walk beat standing by more than double.
Round one: standing beats sitting. A little.
Round two: walking hits more than twice as hard.
Why? Working leg muscles pull sugar out of your blood.
In one lab study, two-minute strolls every twenty minutes
cut the sugar spike by about a quarter.
Short lab studies, though. Not a diabetes treatment.
So after lunch, stroll two minutes every half hour.
