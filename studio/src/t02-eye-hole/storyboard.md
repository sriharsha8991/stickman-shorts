# T02 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #13, slate v4)
Format PLAY · 32.8 s · two places (both announced): a pencil living room at night (sofa, floor lamp, moon in the window) and "inside your eye" (a top-view pencil drawing of an eye, reached by an iris through Spark's glasses). Accent: indigo, on the right dot only (and its ray, ring and the disc). Spark in full colour on the sofa under a blanket with his phone; Mr. Trick on the sofa arm.
Motion language: Playful, calm (night). One transition (the iris). No power-up.
