# H17 facts (checked 2026-11-04)

| Claim on screen / in script | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Part of your attention stays on an unfinished task when you switch ("attention residue"), and performance on the next task suffers | Confirmed. Two experiments. | Leroy 2009, Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes 109(2):168-181. Abstract: https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jobhdp:v:109:y:2009:i:2:p:168-181 |
| Interrupted people kept thinking about task A during task B, and did task B worse | Confirmed (Study 2, word-association measure). | Leroy & Glomb 2018, Organization Science 29(3):380-397. Abstract: https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ororsc:v:29:y:2018:i:3:p:380-397 ; UW news: https://www.washington.edu/news/2018/01/16/task-interrupted-a-plan-for-returning-helps-you-move-on/ |
| Students wrote a short plan before leaving the task: where to resume, what is left | Confirmed (Studies 3 and 4: 66 and 44 students; "ready-to-resume plan"). | UW news + ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2018/01/180116151600.htm |
| "One minute" | Confirmed as the authors' description: the plan need not be long, about a minute is enough. The exact time given in each lab study was not checked in the full paper. | UW news, ScienceDaily |
| The new task went better | Confirmed: less residue, better decisions, more recalled from the résumés they reviewed. | Same |

## Caveats kept
- Small tests with students: said in the script ("with students") and on screen.
- The plan helped the NEW task. The return to the old task was not tested: on screen in the source line.
- The email + boss story is our own everyday example, not the study task (lab: time-limited task, then reviewing résumés). On screen.
- The rope is a picture, said out loud as "like on a rope".

## Dropped
- Row's "up to 40%" (Meyer's spoken estimate, APA): dropped, one idea + no number needed.
- Row's "even finishing isn't enough" (Leroy 2009): true, but a second idea; left out.
- Full papers were not read (paywalled); abstracts + the University of Washington release were used. No effect sizes are shown.
