# V029 storyboard — beat map + shot list (promise → build → reveal)
Series: Designed to get you · 34.9 s · ONE world ~2.5 screens wide, one moving belt connecting two places, one camera rig.
Place A x≈0–1200: crimson belt restaurant (paper lanterns, cup shelf, wood counter). Place B x≈1200–2300: kitchen hatch at the head of the same belt, grey roll door, two metal hands below it.
Motion language: Playful but calm. No power-up, no transition. Crimson + cream, belt-driven: differs from H16 (tangerine + mint).
