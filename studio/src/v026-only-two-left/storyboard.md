# V026 storyboard · "Only 2 left!" (31.1 s, slate v3 #4, Designed to get you)

Motion language: **Energetic** (snappy pushes, ticking clock, one big reveal). Two worlds, both new to the channel:
**A** a night-market gift shop (indigo night, string lights, red neon sign ONLY 2 LEFT!, a tiny cubby with two magenta
gift boxes, a purple roller shutter behind it, a purple shopkeeper labelled SHOP) and **B** a 1970s test kitchen
(peach + mustard wallpaper, avocado counter, two glass cookie jars). Different from V102 (sunny mint/butter
apartment, pizzicato F major) and V025 (plum cinema). Metaphor: **the shop's "last two" are the front of a full
warehouse**; the cookie jars are the proof. Spark scale 1.9, wallet in hand. No power-up (frame 0 must read clean).
One transition: **iris** into the kitchen (V102 shutter, V025 slice, V101 whip).
