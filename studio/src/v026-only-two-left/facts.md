# V026 · "Only 2 left!" — facts (verified 2026-10-28)

| Claim in the video | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| In a test, people rated cookies from a jar of 2 as more desirable than the same cookies from a jar of 10 ("people wanted the two more") | Confirmed. Worchel, Lee & Adewole 1975, Exp. 1: 146 female psychology students (134 analysed); scarce cookies rated more desirable than abundant ones (liking + attractiveness scales) | Worchel, Lee & Adewole (1975), *JPSP* 32(5):906–914 · PDF: https://uni-muenster.de/imperia/md/content/psyifp/aeechterhoff/vorlesungkommunikation/worchelleeeta_suppdemanobjval_jpsp1975.pdf |
| "Nothing changed but the count" | Confirmed: same cookies, same jars; only the number differed | same |
| When the jar went from 10 to 2 in front of them, they wanted it even more | Confirmed: abundant→scarce change rated higher than constant scarcity (strongest when the drop was blamed on demand: other people taking them) | same (abstract + Exp. 1 means) |
| India's consumer regulator bans fake "only X left" claims; it's called false urgency | Confirmed. CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023 (notified 30 Nov 2023, 13 dark patterns, in force). False urgency = "falsely stating or implying the sense of urgency or scarcity…"; illustration (ii): "stating that quantities of a particular product or service are more limited than they actually are". The guidelines say no platform shall engage in any dark pattern. Policy-check 2026: still in force; CCPA penalties incl. a false-urgency case reported in Rajya Sabha (Aug 2026); Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules 2026 adopted 9 Sep 2026, in force 1 Jan 2027, tighten dark-pattern rules further | https://www.verdictum.in/news/central-consumer-protection-authority-dark-patterns-guidelines-notification-1507072 · https://www.aninews.in/news/business/govt-says-ccpa-imposes-rs-20-lakh-for-dark-patterns-steps-up-action-against-deceptive-online-practices20260806161831/ · https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/21426-prohibition-of-dark-patterns-in-consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026 |
