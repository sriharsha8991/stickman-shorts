# T32 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #7, slate v4)
Format STORY · 28.5 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent: cobalt blue on Spark's car = YOU (real road and computer test). Spark in full colour in a small open car; Mr. Trick in his own open car behind him.
Motion language: Playful. No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 0 | CU Spark glaring, in lane 1, a lane-2 car rolls past behind him; painted 1 and 2 on the road; overlay MY LANE IS SLOW; horn |
| 0.2–3.0 | Hook | "Why does your lane lose the race to lane 2?" Point at lane 2 on "lane 2". |
| 4.0–5.5 | Promise | "Wait for the last part." Pull back to the whole road: the city, a big blank roadside screen, Mr. Trick in the open car behind Spark. |
| 6.0–9.1 | Build / first payoff | "You change lanes, and then, your old lane moves." Camera closer. Spark swerves into the gap in lane 2 (anticipation, tilt, overshoot); lane 2 stops; lane 1 starts: Mr. Trick drives past and waves (brass call). Spark recoils, then hands on hips. |
| 9.8–13.3 | Build | "Scientists tested this, with two lanes on a computer." Up to the screen: a scientist walks in with a laptop, cable to the screen; on "computer" the screen lights: COMPUTER TEST, two lanes from above, the blue YOU car. |
| 13.8–16.1 | Build | "Both lanes had the same average speed." SAME AVERAGE SPEED. Spark: chin, thinking. |
| 16.1–17.6 | Pause | 1.5 s, slow push into the screen, Spark leans in and grows a little. |
| 17.6–20.7 | ANSWER | "But other cars passed you for longer than you passed them!" A tight bunch of three cars crawls past YOU (dashed box on the car beside YOU), THEY PASSED YOU bar fills; half-second music drop, hit on "them!" (20.4 s): LONGER stamp, bulb on, PASSED FOR LONGER. |
| 21.9–24.3 | REVEAL | "The cars that you pass are gone quickly." YOU zips past three far-apart cars; YOU PASSED THEM bar stops short (true 43 : 33), SHORTER. Biggest chord at 24.3 s: Spark mindblown. Held to 25.5 s. |
| 25.5–27.4 | Question | "Do you change lanes too often?" Back to Spark; on "change lanes" he swerves back into lane 1 (again), the screen switches off, lane 2 starts moving. |
| 27.4–28.5 | Loop | CU, lane-2 car rolling past behind him, MY LANE IS SLOW = frame 0. |

Promise check: "Wait for the last part" pays off with the computer test showing why the other lane looks faster. True, and it answers the hook.

## Camera keys (rig)
Spark CU (620,1150,×1.8) → whole road (540,980,×1) → lanes (620,1190,×1.5), lane change in the hold → screen + scientist (540,880,×1) → push (560,600,×1.15), still through the reveal → Spark (630,1060,×1.45) → frame 0.

## Spark acting
hands on hips, angry → point at lane 2 → arms crossed, skeptical → point up, wink → hands on wheel, determined → arms crossed, smug → recoil + head shake, shock → hands on hips, angry → point at the scientist, curious → point at the screen → chin, thinking + nod → lean in, curious → point up, worried → recoil + small hop, shock, bulb on → present both, excited → point up → hands at temples, mindblown → lean in, curious → double point, wink → hands on wheel, determined → hands on hips, angry (frame 0).

## Sound
Rush-hour car-radio tune, F major, 108 BPM: electric piano, fingered bass, clean guitar chops, closed hi-hat. Brass call as Mr. Trick drives past, vibraphone when the screen switches on, slow low bass steps while the cars crawl past, half a second of silence, timpani + brass + glockenspiel on "them!", a fast vibraphone run as YOU passes, the biggest chord on the mindblown, groove back, ends on F for the loop. SFX: horns, whooshes on the lane changes, scribble on the screen, impact + stamp on the hit.
