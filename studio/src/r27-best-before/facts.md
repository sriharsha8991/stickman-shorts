# R27 · Is food bad after its best-before date? — facts (verified 16 Nov 2026)

Verdict: **MYTH** — a best-before date is about quality, not safety. Food stored as the label says is still safe after it,
until it shows signs of spoiling (smell, taste, texture). Use-by dates are different: they are about safety.
Education, not medical or food-safety advice for a specific product.

| Line | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | US agencies say best-before dates are about quality, not safety | TRUE | USDA FSIS, "Food Product Dating": "Except for infant formula, dates are not an indicator of the product's safety and are not required by Federal law." FDA, "Working with the Food Industry to Reduce Confusion Over Date Labels" (FDA Voices, 2019): "Best If Used By" "helps consumers understand that the date label is about quality, not safety." |
| 3 | Stored well, it's still safe after the date, just less fresh | TRUE (with the spoilage caveat) | USDA FSIS: "a product should still be safe and wholesome if handled properly until the time spoilage is evident." FSANZ, "Best-before and use-by dates": "You can still eat foods for a while after the best-before date as they should be safe but they may have lost some quality." Codex CXS 1-1985 §2 (best before): "beyond the date the food may still be perfectly satisfactory." |
| 4 | Date mix-ups cause about a fifth of home food waste | TRUE (estimate) | FDA (same page): confusion over date labels "accounts for about 20 percent of food waste in the home". |
| 5 | Bad the next day? Myth! | TRUE | As lines 2–3. |
| 6 | Use-by dates are about safety; never go past them | TRUE | FSANZ: "Foods should not be eaten after the use-by date" — they "can't legally be sold after this date" because they "may pose a health or safety risk." Codex CXS 1-1985 §2 defines use-by / expiration date separately. (The US has no federal use-by rule except infant formula, which must not be used after its date: FSIS.) |

Caveats said in the description: check smell, look and texture; infant formula is the exception; "use by" means safety;
a change in colour of meat is not by itself a sign of spoilage (FSIS).
