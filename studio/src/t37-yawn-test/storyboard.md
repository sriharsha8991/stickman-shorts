# T37 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #2, slate v4)
Format PLAY · 26.9 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent colour: pink on open mouths (and the heart). Spark in full colour; Mr. Trick sets the trap.
World: a pencil office after lunch. A queue for the coffee machine runs towards the camera, a wall screen, a window with the sun, a clock at two.
Motion language: Playful, sleepy. No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0–0.2 | Frame 0 | CU Spark mid-yawn, overlay DO NOT YAWN, sleepy person at the edge. |
| 0.2–3.2 | Hook | "Stop. Can you stop this yawn from reaching your mouth?" Palm out, points at you. |
| 3.6–5.2 | Promise | "Wait for the last part." Pull back to the office, a tall hat rises behind the machine. |
| 6.0–8.9 | Trap | "Watch Spark yawn. Try hard not to yawn." Mr. Trick jumps out and yawns at Spark. Spark crosses his arms, smug, shakes his head, then fights it. Camera pushes to his face. |
| 8.9–12.1 | THE TEST | No voice, camera locked, lines stop moving. Spark's yawn: 0.9 s open, 1.3 s full, 0.7 s close, with a breath sound. |
| 12.1–13.9 | Check | "Did you feel a yawn coming?" Sheepish, shrug. Small line: not everyone yawns. |
| 13.9–14.7 | Pause + drop | Camera travels to the whole queue. Music silent. |
| 14.7–17.5 | Build | "In one big study, about two people in three" Screen comes on: A YAWN VIDEO. Pink mouths open one by one towards the camera, two people stay shut. |
| 17.3–17.9 | ANSWER / REVEAL | "yawned!" hit on the nearest big face, overlay 2 IN 3 YAWNED, Spark mindblown, bulb on for the first time. |
| 17.9–20.5 | Held | Best picture held about 3 s. |
| 19.4–22.9 | Caveat | "Many people say it shows you care. But the proof is weak." Mr. Trick lifts a pink heart IT SHOWS YOU CARE; mouths close; heart goes soft, sinks; WEAK PROOF. Spark laughs into his hand. |
| 23.5–25.8 | Question | "Did you yawn while watching this video?" Close, points, wink. |
| 25.8–26.9 | Loop | His mouth opens into frame 0. |

Promise check: the last part shows the yawn reaching the nearest face, and Spark asks if it reached you. It is true, but it is the weakest score: the payoff depends on the viewer feeling it.

## Spark acting
yawn → palm + shock → point + curious → double point + excited → finger up + smug → arms crossed → hands on hips, determined → arms crossed, smug, head shake → worried, trembling → YAWN → sad → think, worried → shrug, curious → present, thinking → double point, shock → mindblown + hop + bigger + bulb → happy → point, curious → shrug, skeptical → hand to face, laugh → lean in → double point, wink → yawn.

## Sound
Synth breath in + voiced breath out in the test. Pop per mouth, tick on the screen, drop before the reveal, impact + trombone/tuba/timpani on "yawned!", falling trombone on "weak".
