# T34 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #11, slate v4)
Format STORY · 29.7 s · ONE world (a morning street: a city bus cut open at its stop, right in front of an open-plan office), one camera rig, pencil look. Accent: bubble-gum pink (the song's notes, the music box, the gum). Spark in full colour; Mr. Trick lives in a thought bubble over Spark's head and turns the crank of a tiny music box (he keeps the song playing). Light from the bus and office windows.
Motion language: Playful. No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 0 | CU Spark on the bus, six pink notes spin round his head, he swats them; overlay STUCK IN YOUR HEAD |
| 0.2–3.3 | Hook | "Can you stop that one song, stuck inside your head?" Swats, then hands to his head on "stuck". |
| 3.3–4.6 | Pull back | Camera out to the bus; the thought bubble pops open: Mr. Trick cranks a pink music box. |
| 4.7–6.5 | Promise | "Wait until the last part." Mr. Trick tips his hat; Spark points at the bubble. |
| 7.6–9.8 | Build 1 (context) | The bus slows to its stop; the ceiling speaker plays; three notes fly into his ear; he bobs along. |
| 9.9–12.8 | Build 2 (layer) | The camera follows him off the bus into the office ("At the office"); he covers his ears, the notes keep spinning: STILL PLAYING. |
| 13.1–16.9 | Build 3 (example) | Camera out to the whole office: on "most people had this" a note pops over four of five co-workers; MOST PEOPLE, EVERY WEEK. |
| 16.9–18.0 | Pause | Lights + music drop, camera in on Spark and the co-worker (arrives before the line). |
| 18.1–21.6 | ANSWER / REVEAL | "In lab tests": he turns, she tosses a pink gum stick in an arc into his open mouth on "gum" (CHEWING GUM); he chews, the crank slows, five notes fall to the floor one by one; hit on "less!" 21.2 s: bulb lights, mindblown; THE SONG CAME BACK LESS; one note keeps turning slowly (less, not gone), held to ~23 s. |
| 23.1–25.3 | Caveat | "But those tests were small, and quick." SMALL, QUICK TESTS; Mr. Trick starts cranking again, slowly. |
| 26.4–28.6 | Question | CU Spark, double point + wink: "Which song is stuck inside your head?" |
| 28.6–29.7 | Loop | The notes lift off the floor and spin again; overlay STUCK IN YOUR HEAD; Spark swats = frame 0. |

Promise check: "Wait until the last part" pays off with the gum reveal (notes drop, Beaman et al. 2015).

## Camera keys (rig)

## Spark acting (moods: shock, worried, angry, determined, smug, curious, happy, neutral, skeptical, sad, thinking, mindblown, excited, wink)
swat ×5, shock → hands to head, worried → hands on hips + head shake, angry → point up at the bubble, determined → arms crossed, smug → lean in to the speaker, curious → bob to the song, happy → walk → hands to head (ears), worried + shake → arms crossed, skeptical → point at co-workers, curious → shrug, shock → slump, sad → chin, thinking → turn, curious → recoil, mouth open (gum) → chewing, hands on hips, determined → watches the notes fall → mindblown + bulb → celebrate, excited → chin, skeptical → palm "small" → shrug → double point, happy → wink → recoil → swat = frame 0.

## Sound
Morning commute: A major, 112 BPM, Rhodes chords, plucked bass, maracas, an invented four-bar earworm motif on steel drums in the gaps (never a real song). Glockenspiel run for the bus speaker, drop to silence 16.9–18.0 s, marimba slide on the gum toss, the motif winds down slower and lower like a music box running out, timpani + brass + glockenspiel hit on "less!", calm bed with one slow motif note, the motif returns at full speed for the loop. SFX: swish (swats), pop (bubble, co-workers' notes, gum), ding, clink (bus door), whooshes on camera moves, ticks as the notes land, impact + stinger on the hit, riser into the loop.
