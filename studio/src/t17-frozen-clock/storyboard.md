# T17 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #4, slate v4)
Format PLAY · 28.3 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent colour: red on the second hand, the bar and the badge. Spark in full colour; Mr. Trick draws Spark's eyes away, then fills the gap.
World: a pencil office in the last minute of the day. Left: window with a low sun (the light), Spark's desk with closed laptop and packed bag, wall clock at 4:59, filing cabinet. Right along the same wall: stepladder, whiteboard under a pendant lamp, co-worker's desk.
Motion language: Playful, patient waiting. No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 0 | CU Spark in shock, pointing at the clock, hand stuck, overlay THE CLOCK STOPPED? |
| 0.2–2.7 | Hook | "Why do you see 1 clock stop, when you look?" Hand hangs (exaggerated, said later), no ticks. |
| 3.1–4.6 | Promise | "Wait for the last part." Pull back, hand ticks, tall hat rises behind the cabinet. |
| 5.2–8.2 | Test | "Look away from this clock. Then quickly look back." Clock fills the frame, LOOK UP HERE + dot at the top until "look". |
| 8.2–11.1 | Test pause | Only the red hand moves, exact 1 s ticks, no music, no sound, no boil. |
| 11.1–13.0 | Build | "The first second can feel too long." Pull back. Mr. Trick pops up and waves, Spark looks, an eye jumps from him to the clock, one tick is skipped, IT STOPPED! |
| 13.2–15.0 | Fooled | "But the clock never stopped." Co-worker holds up a stopwatch with the same ticks, IT NEVER STOPPED, facepalm. |
| 15.1–16.0 | Drop | Music silent, room dims, Spark runs along the wall to the whiteboard, camera follows. |
| 16.0–18.9 | ANSWER | "In a lab, under one second felt like one full second!" LAB TEST, red bar REALLY ON SCREEN 0.88 s, dark bar FELT LIKE grows. |
| 18.9–21.4 | REVEAL | Hit on "second!": 1 FULL SECOND slams in, dashed gap shows at the start of the red bar, mindblown, bulb on for the first time. Held 2.5 s. |
| 21.4–24.9 | Why | "Your eyes jump. Your brain may then fill that moment." Eye hops over the gap (EYES JUMP), Mr. Trick scribbles it red (FILLED IN?). Bars match. |
| 25.6–27.2 | Question | "Have you seen this happen?" Back at the desk, double point, wink. |
| 27.2–28.3 | Loop | Points at the clock, hand stuck on the frame-0 second, shock: frame 0. |

Promise check: the last part shows the red bar shorter than the second it felt like, and the gap it hides. True, and it pays off the stop in the hook.
