# Your streak is a hostage — storyboard (V003)

9:16 · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · 1080 frames (36 s) · no voiceover: kinetic word captions carry the script.
Format: **Number Reveal**. Music 120 BPM → 1 bar = 60 frames; every scene starts on a bar line.

## Direction (motion-art-direction)
- Personality: **Energetic** (tug-of-war physics, heavy snaps, a big hit on the number).
  Base unit 5 frames, SNAP entrances, caption stagger 3 frames.
- Palette: warm sunrise paper (#fbe6c8) + flame oranges/yellows; YOU = blue (the flame owns orange).
  Twist scene goes cold (ice blue tint). Different from V001 (desk/code) and V002 (night bed / slot machine).
- Metaphor: the streak is a living flame on a leash. Day 1 you walk it; day 247 it drags you.
- One "wow": the balance scale — "+1 day" vs "−247" — the counter climbs 1.0× → 2× with a riser and
  slams the loss pan to the floor on bar 7 (frame 420).
- Interrupts every 2–4 s (uneven): slams, punch-ins, counter, stamp, freeze, cards, notification.

## Hook (three layers)
- First frame (muted): a giant flame with "247" on its belly yanking a stick figure across the ground by a leash.
- Overlay: "DAY 247." (visible at frame 0) + "DON'T BREAK IT." slams at frame 8.
- Caption (hookscore 70 WORKABLE, The Question): "You open the app before coffee. / Why does losing a streak hurt / more than starting one felt good?"
