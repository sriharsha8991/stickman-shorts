# T26 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #5, slate v4)
Format STORY · 30.5 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent: lamp yellow on the cone, the shirt, the people who looked, the badge. Spark in full colour; Mr. Trick walks the roof with the lamp.
World: a building in cut-away. Left: office lift (floor 4, buttons, four people). Door. Right: raked test room with three rows of four students at desks, a wall switch beside the door.
Motion language: Playful, awkward then relieved. No power-up, no transition.

## Sound

Bass pulse + growing strings as the eyes open, silence for the switch, three pizzicato steps on one in four, timpani + brass + glockenspiel on "noticed".
