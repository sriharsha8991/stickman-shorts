# T11 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #6, slate v4)
Format PLAY · 30.9 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent: pea green on the pea, the ghost peas and the two touched finger sides. Spark in full colour, toy-sized on a family dinner table. The huge hand is the viewer's own, palm side, from the top of the frame.
Motion language: Playful. No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 0 | CU Spark in shock, huge crossed fingers on one pea, dashed ghost pea beside it, overlay CROSS YOUR FINGERS |
| 0.2–3.0 | Hook | "Why do your 2 fingers feel what is never there?" |
| 3.6–5.1 | Promise | "Wait for the last part." Pull back: the family, the table, Mr. Trick rises at the front edge. Hand opens and lifts. |
| 5.6–8.5 | Build | "Cross two fingers on one hand, just like this." Slow crossing, Spark points. |
| 9.0–12.2 | Build | "Now, touch a pea, or your nose, with both tips." Hand comes down, grandfather tries his nose, A PEA OR YOUR NOSE. |
| 12.2–15.2 | TEST | Camera holds. TRY IT NOW, three dots fill, Closed eyes make it stronger. Spark out of the test area, sound almost off. |
| 15.2–17.2 | Question | "Does that one thing feel like two?" Pull back, ghost pea pops on "two". Spark is sure, runs to the napkin and looks under it. Nothing. Mr. Trick grins. |
| 18.4–22.2 | ANSWER | "Normally, those two sides of your fingers touch two different things!" Green marks on the two sides, fingers uncross, marks travel apart, salt + pepper slide in. Hit on "things!" at 21.8 s, bulb on for the first time, NORMALLY TWO THINGS. |
| 23.0–26.0 | REVEAL | "So, your brain seems to decide there are two things." Fingers cross back on the one pea, half a second of silence, hit on "things." at 25.6 s: two dashed peas pop out of the marks, YOUR BRAIN SAYS TWO, Spark's arms up + hop + bigger. Held to 27.9 s. |
| 27.9–29.8 | Question | "Did you feel two, or one?" Lean in, double point, two, one. |
| 29.8–30.9 | Loop | Back to frame 0, ghost pea, CROSS YOUR FINGERS. |

Promise check: "Wait for the last part" pays off with why the fingers report two things. True, and it answers the hook.

## Spark acting

## Sound
After-dinner tune, G major, 92 BPM: felt piano, accordion, pizzicato bass, flute answers. Almost silent in the test with three soft ticks. Rising notes as the fingers uncross, timpani + brass + glockenspiel on "things!", half a second of silence, the biggest hit on "things.". Ends on frame 0's chord.
