# V103 storyboard · "Your pocket buzzed. Your phone didn't." (20.0 s, slate v3 #5, Your brain did this)

Motion language: **Playful** (snappy jolts, wobbling grab straps, one alarm-room gag). Two worlds, both new to the channel:
**A** a sunny morning metro carriage (coral seats, lemon grab rails, sky windows with a city skyline sliding past,
three green-scrubs commuters labelled STAFF) and **B** inside Spark's head: a **sonar room** called BUZZ DETECTOR
(dark olive walls, amber radar scope, red alarm lamp, a SENSITIVITY dial jammed at MAX). Different from V026 (indigo
night market, lo-fi Rhodes D minor) and V102 (mint dollhouse). Metaphor: **your brain is a sonar operator listening
for a buzz, with the sensitivity cranked up**: clothing and twitches show up as blips and set off the alarm.
Spark scale 1.9, phone lives in his hip pocket. No power-up (frame 0 must read clean). One transition: **whip** into
the sonar room (V026 iris, V102 shutter, V025 slice).
