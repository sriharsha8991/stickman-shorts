You paid two hundred rupees. Here's your scratch card.
Better luck next time.
So why do you scratch the next one?
Some cards pay. Some are empty.
And you never know which one is next.
In the fifties, a psychologist tested this on pigeons.
Random rewards kept them pecking the longest,
even after the food stopped coming.
Same pull that keeps slot machines busy.
The empty card isn't a glitch.
The rules even say not every card wins.
That maybe is the hook.
So pick your app for the payment, not the scratch.
Now, about that next card...
