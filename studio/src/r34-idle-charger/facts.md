# R34 · Does an empty charger in the socket cost you money? — facts (verified 18 Nov 2026)

Verdict: **MYTH** — a modern phone charger left in the socket with nothing attached draws almost nothing. EU and US
rules cap the no-load ("idle") power of a phone-sized charger at 0.1 W. Left in all year, that is
0.1 W × 8,760 h = 0.876 kWh, i.e. under one unit (1 kWh) of electricity a year — about what a 9 W LED bulb uses
in four days left on (9 W × 96 h = 0.864 kWh). Caveat in plain words: older chargers were allowed more
(EU 0.5 W in 2010, 0.3 W from 2011, no EU limit before), and a charger that feels warm with nothing attached is
drawing power, so unplug those.
