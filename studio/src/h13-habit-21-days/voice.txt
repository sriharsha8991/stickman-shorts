You quit your new habit on day twenty-two?
The twenty-one day rule was never about habits.
It traces back to a 1960 self-help book.
A surgeon saw patients take about twenty-one days
to adjust to a new face.
When researchers actually tracked habits for twelve weeks,
the median was sixty-six days.
And the range? Eighteen days to two hundred fifty-four.
A glass of water: quick. Fifty sit-ups: slow.
Small study, simple habits. But the best part:
missing one day barely mattered.
So stop counting to twenty-one.
Missed a day? Just show up the next.
