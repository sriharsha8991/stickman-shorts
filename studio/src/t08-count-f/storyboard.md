# T08 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #12, slate v4)
Format PLAY · 30.1 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent: the boss's red pen (the 6, the rings round OF, the Fs inside them). Spark in full colour at the right end of the desk; Mr. Trick on top of the clipboard; the boss (doodle, bun + glasses) walks in from the door on the left.
Motion language: Playful, calm (office morning). No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 0 | CU Spark, smug, arms crossed, next to the typed report on the clipboard; overlay COUNT THE Fs |
| 0.2–3.2 | Hook | "How many letters will you miss before your boss does?" Points at the report on "letters", shrugs on "miss", worried on "boss". |
| 3.8–5.3 | Promise | "Wait for the last part." Pull back to the desk; Mr. Trick pops up behind the clipboard and tips his hat. |
| 5.7–8.1 | Build | "Count every letter F in this report." Camera onto the report. |
| 6.3–14.6 | TEST | Fully still picture (no camera, no Spark in the test area, still pencil lines), sound almost silent: the sentence FINISHED FILES ARE / THE RESULT OF YEARS / OF SCIENTIFIC STUDY / COMBINED WITH THE / EXPERIENCE OF YEARS. |
| 14.6–17.0 | Spark fooled | "Spark counted three, and he was sure." Back to the desk; three pencil ticks under the Fs of FINISHED, FILES, SCIENTIFIC; bubble 3; smug nod. The boss walks in. |
| 17.5–19.2 | ANSWER | "But his boss found six!" She picks up the red pen and writes a red 6 on the report; hit on "six!" (18.7 s), ring round the 6, Spark's 3 crossed out; THERE ARE 6; Spark recoil + shock → facepalm. |
| 19.2–20.2 | Pause | Half-second music drop; slow push in on the report and Spark. |
| 20.2–22.4 | REVEAL | "The small word of is there three times." A red ring round each OF on "of", "there", "times." (20.9 / 21.4 / 21.9 s); each F inside turns red. Big chord on "times." (22.0 s): Spark mindblown, bulb lights, Mr. Trick falls behind the clipboard. 3 Fs HIDE IN OF, held to ~24.5 s. |
| 23.4–26.7 | Takeaway | "Readers often miss letters in small, common words." Pull back to the desk; source + caveat line small at the bottom. |
| 27.2–29.1 | Question | "How many Fs did you count?" Back to Spark's face, double point + wink. |
| 29.1–30.1 | Loop | Smug, arms crossed, COUNT THE Fs = frame 0. |

Promise check: "Wait for the last part" pays off with the reveal of where the three missed Fs were (all in OF). True.

## Spark acting

## Sound
E major 104 BPM office groove: staccato piano off-beats, pizzicato bass, woodblock "typewriter" ticks, comic bassoon in the gaps. The test is near silent (one held pad note, nothing rhythmic). Celesta ticks for Spark's three, bassoon on "sure", pizz + low brass stab on "six!", silence 19.3–20.1 s, three rising celesta + brass notes on the rings, timpani + brass + glockenspiel on "times.", calm bed under the takeaway, groove back into frame 0. SFX: whoosh on camera moves, boing (Mr. Trick), scribble (ticks, the 6, rings), clink (pen), impact + stamp on "six!", ding on the reveal.
