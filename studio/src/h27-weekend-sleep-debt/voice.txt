You think your weekend lie-in pays back five-hour nights?
Your body disagrees.
In a lab, people slept just five hours a night.
One group got to sleep in all weekend.
Then Monday came, and the short nights came back.
Did the lie-in fix it? Nope.
Insulin sensitivity, how well you handle sugar,
still fell, by up to twenty-seven percent.
They snacked after dinner and gained weight,
and their body clocks drifted later.
The yo-yo itself may be the problem.
Small study: thirty-six young adults, two weeks.
So protect a steady sleep window on weekdays.
Before Monday's alarm rings again.
