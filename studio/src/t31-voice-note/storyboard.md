# T31 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #1, slate v4)
Format STORY · ~27.2 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent colour: orange on the phone's waveform and the air path. Spark in full colour; Mr. Trick's first appearance.
World: a pencil living room. Sofa + sleeping cat, side table with the phone, tall cabinet on the right (Mr. Trick hides behind it), a rolled paper sheet on the wall with a pull cord. The sheet unrolls in the same room: no change of place.
Motion language: Playful. No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2–3.4 | Hook | Frame 0: CU Spark, hands on his head, shock; phone on the table playing a voice note. Overlay THAT IS YOU? "You never sound right on your own recordings, but why?" |
| 3.8–5.3 | Promise | "Wait for the last part." Pull back to the room; palm out, finger up; a tall hat rises behind the cabinet. |

| 12.9–15.9 | Layer | Mr. Trick pulls the cord, the sheet unrolls: a head drawing. "You hear yourself through air (orange path round the head to the ear), and your bones (a big bone inside the head)." Spark: easy, counts them; bulb flickers. Phone slapped on the sheet. |
| 15.9–16.9 | Pause + drop | Room dims, sheet stays lit, music drops. Eraser pops out of Mr. Trick's hat; he walks along the rod. |
| 16.9–19.3 | REVEAL | "A recording has only the air part!" Orange wave reaches the phone; on "only" he rubs the bone out, crumbs fall; on "part!" hit, lights back, ONLY AIR!, Spark mindblown, bulb on for the first time. |
| 19.3–20.8 | Held | Still best picture. |

| 26.1–27.2 | Loop | Sheet rolls up taking Mr. Trick with it, phone lands on the table, frame 0. |
