# T35 storyboard — beat map + shot list (Spark Got Tricked #9, slate v4)
Format STORY · 28.9 s · ONE world, one camera rig, no cuts, pencil look. Accent: vermilion on the hidden word card. Spark in full colour at the whiteboard; Mr. Trick on top of the whiteboard holds the word on a string; three doodle co-workers at the table under a wall calendar; the lamp over the table is the light.
Motion language: Playful. No power-up, no transition.

## Beat map
| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 0 | CU Spark, shock, finger at his mouth; an empty word card (7 slots) hangs from his mouth on a string; overlay IT'S ON MY TONGUE |
| 0.2–2.8 | Hook | "Why does your memory hide one word you know?" On "hide" (1.5 s) the card dips, then is yanked up; Spark hops and reaches. |
| 3.0–5.5 | Promise | Pull back: the meeting room, Mr. Trick on the whiteboard holds the card out of reach, tips his hat. "Wait for the last part." Spark points up, determined. |
| 6.3–9.2 | Build 1 | "You know the word, but somehow, you cannot say it." Spark points at his slide (a stapler drawing), jumps on "cannot", Mr. Trick lifts the card; facepalm. |
| 9.9–12.7 | Build 2 | Camera to the table + calendar. Each co-worker gets an empty word card bubble. "about once every week": a mark pops on Wednesday; ABOUT ONCE A WEEK. |
| 13.4–16.0 | Build 3 | "Later": the calendar page turns; camera back; the card comes down to Spark's mouth, then is yanked again on "hide"; THE SAME WORD AGAIN; Spark angry. |
| 16.2–17.0 | Pause | Lights down, music drop, slow push in. |
| 17.0–20.5 | ANSWER / REVEAL | "But when people found it themselves, it hid less often!" Spark's thought bubble flicks letters A? M? B? T? K? → S! (18.3 s), the card shows S, he crouches, jumps, grabs, pulls; the string snaps on "often!" (19.9 s): STAPLER fills in, rays, mindblown + bulb, Mr. Trick falls behind the board; IT HIDES LESS OFTEN, held to 21.5 s. |
| 22.0–25.0 | Action | Camera to Spark + co-worker: she holds up a note with S. "So ask for the first letter, then find the word." |
| 26.0–27.8 | Question | "Which word hides from you most?" Back to Spark's face; he tosses the card away; Mr. Trick lowers a new empty card into his mouth. |
| 28.2–28.9 | Loop | Shock, finger at the mouth, IT'S ON MY TONGUE = frame 0. |

Promise check: "Wait for the last part" pays off with the string snapping when Spark finds the word himself, and the true finding that it hides less often after that.

## Sound

Vibe gliss on each yank, celesta ding on "once", page on the flip, silence for the drop, tremolo strings + wood-block ticks while letters flick, celesta on S!, timpani pickup, then timpani + crash + brass + glockenspiel run on "often!", brass ta-da, lighter groove ending on Dmaj7 for the loop.
