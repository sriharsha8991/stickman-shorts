# V028 · Does your cab cost more when your battery is low? — facts (checked 2026-10-30)

| Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The ride app can see your phone's battery level | Confirmed (Uber's head of economic research, Keith Chen: the app reads battery level to switch to power-saving mode) | NPR Hidden Brain, "This Is Your Brain On Uber" (May 2016), transcript https://www.npr.org/transcripts/478266839 ; WFDD/NPR write-up https://wfdd.org/story/your-brain-uber |
| In 2016 the company said, from its own data, that people with almost empty phones are more willing to accept surge (high) prices | Confirmed, as the company's own statement (not a published study). Chen: "one of the strongest predictors of whether or not you are going to be sensitive to surge ... is how much battery you have left on your cell phone" (below about 5%, when the icon turns red) | NPR transcript above; Engadget 20 May 2016 https://www.engadget.com/2016-05-20-uber-surge-pricing-battery-life.html |
| The company says it does not use battery level to set prices | Confirmed. 2016: "we absolutely don't use that to kind of like push you a higher surge price" (Chen, NPR). Today: Uber's Pricing Principles say it does not use "battery level, or similar technical characteristics as inputs to set prices or promotions" | https://tb-static.uber.com/prod/uber-static/uber-sites/_pdf/ai-on-uber/Pricing_Principles_one-pager.pdf |
| Prices go up when many people want a car (and few drivers are free) | Confirmed (company's description of dynamic pricing: real-time supply and demand, time and distance) | Pricing Principles one-pager; Uber's reply to Brussels claims (Taxi Point) https://www.taxi-point.co.uk/post/uber-rejects-brussels-allegations-of-charging-users-more-if-they-have-low-phone-battery |
| Myth: "the app raises your fare when your battery is low" | Not supported. One small 2023 newspaper test in Brussels (Dernière Heure: two phones, 12% vs 84% battery, quotes €17.56 vs €16.60) was denied by Uber; a single test can't separate battery from other differences. Not used in the video | Taxi Point; Business Today 14 Apr 2023 https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/uber-charges-more-from-users-if-their-phone-battery-is-low-report-claims-377403-2023-04-14 |
| "A low battery can make you rush and say yes" | Kept as the plain-words version of the company's finding (people with low battery accept surge more); not a claim about why | NPR transcript |
