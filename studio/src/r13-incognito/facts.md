| Line in the Short | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Does incognito mode really hide what you browse?" | The belief on trial (hook) | Common belief. |
| "It only stops your own browser from saving history." | Confirmed (plain-words summary) | Chrome Help 95464: "After your Incognito session ends, Chrome doesn't retain site data or a record of the sites you visited." (Also cookies/site data and form info; downloads and bookmarks stay.) |
| "The websites you visit can still see you." | Confirmed | Chrome Help 9845881: "Websites you visit, including Google sites, and organizations that manage your network" may be able to observe your activity in Incognito. |
| "So can your office network and your internet provider." | Confirmed | Same pages name "your school, employer, or internet service provider". |
| "Chrome itself tells you this. So "hidden" is a myth!" | Confirmed | Chrome Help 9845881: "While Incognito can help keep your browsing private on your device, it doesn't make you invisible." |
| "It hides your history from people who share your device." | Confirmed | Chrome Help 95464 / 9845881: Incognito limits what is saved to your device; a shared computer is the example given. Caveat for the description: files you download and bookmarks you save still stay on the device. |
