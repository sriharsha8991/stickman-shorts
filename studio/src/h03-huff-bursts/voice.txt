You never exercise? Three one-minute bursts a day might still count. How?
They hide in things you already do.
Stairs. A dash for the bus. Heavy bags.
Twenty-five thousand non-exercisers wore trackers.
The ones doing about three bursts a day
were linked to about forty percent lower risk of death.
Heart-disease deaths? Nearly half.
One observational study: healthier people may just move more.
So pick your three bursts today.
