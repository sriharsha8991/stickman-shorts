# H06 storyboard — beat map + shot list (promise → build → reveal)
Series tag: MYTH CHECK · 37.1 s · ONE world (2.2 screens tall), one VERTICAL camera rig, no cuts. Announced moves: "Let's climb" → stairs; "try one floor of stairs" → slide down to the lobby.
World: cut-away apartment tower at dusk. x 0–330 lift shaft, x 330–1060 stairwell, four flights zigzag (17 + 17 + 16 = 50 steps, last flight 10 more), roof at y −470, city + street around.
Motion language: Playful. No power-up, no transition. Periwinkle dusk → coral sunrise, brick + sage + slate: differs from V030 (peach + steel lab), H30 (butter kitchen), V029 (crimson).

| s | Beat | What |
|---|---|---|

| 22–24.6 | REVEAL | "heart risk was about one fifth lower!" Top block leaves on "fifth", falls past the tower, smashes on the street (24.4 s). ABOUT 1/5 LOWER. Dashed gap in the stack. Best picture 23.7–25. |
