# H01 · 10,000 steps came from a pedometer, not a study — facts

| Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The 10,000 goal "likely derives from the trade name of a pedometer sold in 1965 … in Japan", Manpo-kei = "10 000 steps meter" | confirmed; script keeps "likely" | Lee et al. (2019), JAMA Internal Medicine 179(8):1105 — https://whish.stanford.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Step-Volume-Intensity-Mortality_JAMA-IntMedicine-2019-.pdf |
