# R33 · Do you need to warm up your car? — facts (verified 18 Nov 2026)

Verdict: **MYTH** — a modern car does not need a long idle to warm up. Even on the coldest days, most manufacturers
recommend driving off gently after about 30 seconds; driving warms the engine faster than idling, and idling only burns
fuel. Caveat in plain words: drive gently for the first few minutes, and follow your own car's manual.

| 4 | Ten idle minutes can burn half a litre (card: 10 min idling · burns 0.25–0.5 L of fuel · NRCan) | TRUE (upper end) | Natural Resources Canada, AutoSmart fact sheet 3: "Ten minutes of idling burns 0.25 to 0.50 litres of fuel" (600–1,200 g CO2). |
